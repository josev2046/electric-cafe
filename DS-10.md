## KORG DS-10 Synthesiser: Quick Reference Guide

### 1. Navigation and Sound Generation
*   **Access Synths:** Tap the top menu tabs to switch between the two analogue synthesisers (SYN1, SYN2) and the four-part drum machine (DRUM).
*   **Tweak Oscillators (VCO):** Adjust the waveform, pitch, and pulse-width on the VCO screen to set the fundamental tone.
*   **Shape the Sound (VCF/VCA):** Navigate to the VCF screen to filter frequencies (cutoff/peak), and use the EG (Envelope Generator) to dictate how the sound's volume and timbre change over time.

### 2. Patching and Modulation
*   **Access the Patch Panel:** Tap the 'Patch' button to open the virtual cable routing interface.
*   **Route Signals:** Drag a virtual cable from an output jack (e.g., LFO triangle wave) to an input jack (e.g., VCO pitch) to create complex modulations.
*   **Adjust Depth:** Use the corresponding dials on the synth interface to dictate how intensely the patched signal affects the target parameter.

### 3. Sequencing and Arranging
*   **Program Steps:** Enter the SEQ screen, select a track, and tap the 16-step grid to place notes or drum hits into the pattern.
*   **Record in Real-Time:** Press the 'Rec' button followed by 'Play', then use the touch-screen keyboard or Kaoss Pad to input notes live.
*   **Arrange Patterns:** Switch to the SONG mode to chain your 16-step patterns together sequentially into a complete track.

### 4. Performance and Effects
*   **Utilise the Kaoss Pad:** Open the KAOSS screen to play notes or manipulate assigned parameters (like filter cutoff) by dragging your stylus across the X/Y grid.
*   **Apply Master Effects:** Route SYN1, SYN2, or DRUM tracks through the FX section to add master chorus, flanger, or delay.
*   **Save Projects:** Access the system menu before powering down to store your patterns and patch configurations locally on the cartridge.
